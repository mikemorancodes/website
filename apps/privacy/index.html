Support Privacy

This page describes general privacy practices for apps developed by Mike Moran. Features and data use can vary between apps, so any app-specific privacy information supplements this page and explains how that app works.

Information and app features

Apps may store information you enter or create while using their features. What information is involved, where it is stored, and whether it is shared will depend on the app. App-specific details will be provided in the app or its store listing when relevant.

Apps may use device features such as notifications or the system share menu. You can manage permissions through your device settings. When you choose to share information, the device or service you select will handle it under its own terms and privacy policy.

DrinkingBuddy

Data stored on your device

DrinkingBuddy stores drink entries, daily records, and app preferences locally on your device. It uses local notifications for reminders. The app does not require an account or send this recorded information to a server operated by Mike Moran.

If you choose to share a progress summary, DrinkingBuddy opens your device’s share interface. The information is then handled by the app or person you choose to share it with.

Contact

If you have a privacy question about one of the apps, email support@mikemorancodes.com. Please identify the app you are asking about.
